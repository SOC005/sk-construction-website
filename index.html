Transparent Workflow

Our Construction Process

Every quality structure follows a disciplined sequence. Here is how we guide your construction project from concept to handover.

01

1. Initial Consultation

We meet to discuss your property location, family vision, preliminary layout thoughts, and project objectives in Chhattisgarh.

02

2. Understanding Requirements

Review of architectural floor plans, structural drawings, municipal guidelines, soil conditions, and specific material preferences.

03

3. Site Visit & Scope Discussion

Thorough on-site inspection of plot boundaries, access roads, water source availability, and electricity logistics before finalizing work scope.

04

4. Estimate & Proposal

Itemized bill of quantities (BOQ) with transparent specification tiers, clearly defined milestone schedules, and agreed contract terms.

05

5. Construction & Progress Updates

Active civil execution with regular milestone reviews, photographic updates, slump test monitoring, and continuous on-site supervision.

06

6. Finishing, Inspection & Handover

Thorough joint quality inspection covering plumbing, electrical points, plaster lines, and tile leveling before formal key handover.

* Note: The exact workflow and milestones are customized according to your specific project requirements, architectural blueprints, and agreed contract.
