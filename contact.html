Consultation FAQ

Common questions when preparing for a residential house construction project in Chhattisgarh.

What should I bring to our first consultation?

Plot dimensions, layout sketches, title boundaries, or any architectural drawings you have. If you do not have drawings yet, we can guide you on the typical room layouts and configurations suited for Chhattisgarh plots.

Do you conduct physical site inspections before quoting?

Yes. A physical site visit is critical to inspect road accessibility, soil firmness, water level, and electric supply lines. We discuss the scope in detail on site before submitting a formal proposal.

How are payments structured during construction?

We operate strictly on milestone-based stages: foundation completion, plinth beam, column casting, slab casting, brickwork, plastering, and finishing. You only pay for verified work completed on your site.
