Our Story

A Decade of Practical Site Experience

Founded on the belief that a construction firm should be defined by what it builds with its own hands, SK Construction has been actively working across Chhattisgarh for over 10 years.

Engineering Principles

How We Build Quality

We do not compromise on the unseen structural core of your building.

Soil-Adapted Foundations

Every area in Chhattisgarh has varying soil profiles. We assess ground bearing conditions to engineer appropriate footing depths, column footings, and plinth tie-beams.

High-Grade Certified Steel

We use corrosion-resistant Fe-500D / Fe-550D primary TMT rebar with certified elongation properties for maximum earthquake resilience and structural stability.

Scientific Curing Protocols

Concrete only attains its design compressive strength when cured continuously. We mandate pond-curing and burlap wrapping for a minimum of 14 to 21 days on all slabs.

Accurate Brickwork & Plumb

We enforce rigorous string-line and plumb-bob verification on all external and internal brick walls, ensuring level plaster and crack-free surfaces.

Integrated Waterproofing

Dampness is the enemy of fine interiors. We apply chemical damp-proof courses (DPC) at plinth level and multi-layer polymer coatings on all terrace and toilet areas.

Transparent Communication

You speak directly with the people running the job. Weekly progress photographs, clear milestone bills, and zero unexpected cost inflations.
