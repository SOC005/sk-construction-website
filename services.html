Service 01

New House Construction

Complete Turnkey Execution from Groundbreaking to Handover

Building a new residential house requires harmonizing soil mechanics, architectural blueprints, steel reinforcement, and masonry craftsmanship. SK Construction manages the full residential lifecycle according to your approved drawings and agreed contractual scope.

Key Scope Highlights:

  • ✓ Precise site layout demarcation and isolated/raft footing excavation
  • ✓ High-strength RCC plinth beams, columns, and vibration-compacted slabs
  • ✓ First-class red brick or fly-ash brick masonry with chemical DPC barriers
  • ✓ Internal and external double-coat plaster and conduit integration

Structural & Civil Work

Heavy Substructure, RCC Framing & High-Tolerance Masonry

The structural framework is the heart of a safe home. We execute specialized civil and structural works—including deep pile footings, retaining walls, heavy column reinforcements, and machine-vibrated slabs.

Key Scope Highlights:

  • ✓ Fe-500D / Fe-550D TMT rebar bending and tying per structural engineer schedules
  • ✓ Calibrated concrete slump testing and machine needle vibration
  • ✓ Reinforced compound walls, water sump tanks, and septic tank civil works
